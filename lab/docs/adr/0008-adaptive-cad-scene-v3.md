# ADR 0008: Adaptive procedural CAD scene v3

## Status

Accepted, 2026-09-12.

## Context

The public 3D view rendered every published piece as the same cube. The exact transform and
membership contract was strong, but roof, stair, pole, portal, terrain, surface-confidence, and
depth cues were discarded. Shipping Valheim meshes, materials, or textures is outside Steward's
redistribution policy.

## Decision

`/api/scene` remains v2 by default. The browser explicitly requests `format=3` and receives
`steward-zdo-scene/v3`, whose payload contains:

- the unchanged 80-byte local transform/color instance stream;
- one anonymous uint32 piece ordinal per render instance; and
- an optional checksummed selection-local terrain vertex crop.

The browser verifies both checksums and rejects a package unless every ordinal is in range and every
selected piece occurs at least once. No persistent object ID or absolute origin is added to the public
package.

Public-cache schema 5 stores a primitive kind, surface class, and confidence on each exact prefab
name/hash geometry row. The approved procedural vocabulary is `box`, `sloped-panel-26`,
`sloped-panel-45`, `triangular-prism`, `stepped-stair`, `cylinder-12`, `arch-12`, `ring-12`, and
`plane-double-sided`. Runtime substring matching is forbidden. Audited compound primitives may carry
the same vocabulary in `steward-prefab-representations/v2`.

Scene assembly selects deterministic LOD:

- detail: at most 5,000 pieces and 40,000 desired instances, all accepted parts, 2 m interpolated
  terrain, 4x MSAA, CAD edges, and a 2048 directional shadow map;
- standard: at most 50,000 pieces and 250,000 desired instances, at most four parts per piece, 8 m
  terrain, CAD edges, one sample, and no shadow map; or
- overview: larger exact selections through 250,000 pieces, one envelope or marker per piece, terrain
  at 32 m or coarser and at most 65,536 vertices, and no shadow map.

Terrain-context schema 3 adds a checksummed row-major little-endian uint16 elevation field with a
256-pixel logical tile size. It represents the same final elevation used for the public raster after
terrain edits. Schema 2 contexts remain readable and scenes fall back honestly to the local grid.

The default presentation is a neutral analysis-first CAD view with family colors, filled geometry,
edge overlays, gamma-aware lighting, terrain/water/contours when available, and explicit LOD,
provenance, resolution, and quality labels.

## Consequences

Native game assets, dynamic door/wear/fire state, collision, interiors, and picking remain out of
scope. Historical terrain generated by a current client remains labeled regenerated and
non-authoritative. Deployments must rebuild context artifacts and public caches to gain terrain and
persisted primitive metadata; older caches continue to render through compatibility fallbacks.
