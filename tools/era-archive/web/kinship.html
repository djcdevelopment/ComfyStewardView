Who you built beside

Every branch is a builder who placed pieces on the same builds as the anchor, era by era, read from the saved world. Tags are what the build's majority owner adds on top.

    Solid branches carry the pieces you placed side by side in that era. Dashed branches are eras kept only as photographs, where shares are unknown. A hairline is an era apart; the branch resumes when you built together again.

    What a tag is

    A kinship tag is self-reported. It is recorded on this device and needs sending; credited builders come from the saved world and a tag never changes them. Coordinator-confirmed tags are published from participation.json.
