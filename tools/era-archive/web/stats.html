3. The Album Inflation Factor & Player Archetypes

Distribution percentiles across all {{builders}} recorded builders ({{n_with_pieces}} carry a piece count; a legacy gallery credit counts as an album but not as pieces). Because the spatial clustering algorithm guarantees zero piece loss by grouping even isolated single pieces into builds, active players accumulate massive album inflation.

{{table_3_body}}
Metric Min 10% 25% Median (50%) 75% 90% 95% Max Mean

The Trailblazer / Nomad

Explores broad swathes of wilderness. Accumulates hundreds or thousands of single-piece albums (campfires, repair benches, portals, torches) with an inflation factor over 50x.

The Homesteader

Concentrates almost all building into 1 to 3 core bases. High lead ownership (>80%), moderate album counts, and a low inflation ratio near 2x to 4x.

The Community Tourist

Travels between established community towns and fortresses, adding small personal touches (chests, crafting stations, beds) with under 5% individual share.

The Long-Term Pioneer

Server veterans active across 4+ eras. Balances deep wilderness exploration trails (300+ utility albums) with 10–25 substantial landmark fortresses.
